Readiness check

Eight questions, one minute. Answer honestly; "partly" is the most common answer we hear.

A senior leader owns AI decisions, budget and risk.
The data for our top use case is accessible and we know who controls it.
At least one engineer can integrate an API into our systems and deploy it.
We know which regulations apply to our AI use (DPDP Act, EU AI Act, sector rules).
