Engagement · Assessment

Two to four weeks to a roadmap you can fund

An assessment is the lowest-risk way to start. We interview stakeholders, examine your data and systems, score candidate use cases and return a written roadmap with estimates, so your next decision is a budget decision, not a leap of faith.

Duration
2 to 4 weeks
Pricing
Fixed fee, agreed before we start
Team
One senior consultant lead plus a data or compliance specialist
Book an assessment

Who it's for

Leaders who need a ranked, costed view before committing budget.

  • Leaders deciding where to invest first
  • Teams with a list of ideas and no ranking
  • Regulated firms needing a compliance gap view

What happens

A tight sequence with leadership time protected.

  • Week 1: interviews and system walkthroughs
  • Weeks 2 to 3: data review, scoring and estimates
  • Final week: roadmap readout with leadership

What you get

Written deliverables you can circulate and fund.

  • Scored use-case portfolio with owners
  • 12 to 18 month roadmap and budget
  • Risk, data and governance findings

What it leads to

A clear next step, including the option to wait.

  • A fixed-scope build on the top use case
  • A fractional leadership arrangement
  • Or an honest recommendation to hold
