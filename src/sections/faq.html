Questions we get on the first call

How quickly can we start, and what does the first month look like?

Usually within two weeks of a signed scope. The first two to four weeks are an assessment: interviews, a look at your data and systems, and a written roadmap with estimates. You can stop there or move straight into a build.

Do you work on fixed fees or time and materials?

Fixed fees for assessments and build phases, with written scope and acceptance criteria. Managed operations and fractional roles are monthly retainers you can end with notice. We avoid open-ended time-and-materials work.

Which models and clouds do you use?

Whichever fit the use case. We work on Azure, AWS and GCP, with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and open-weight models, and we self-host when data residency requires it. We are not paid by any vendor to recommend them.

Can you help if our data isn't ready?

Yes. Data readiness is one of our service lines, and most programmes include some of it. We will tell you honestly if the data gap is big enough that a build should wait.

Do you handle EU AI Act and other regulatory work for companies outside Europe?

Yes. Indian and Middle East companies selling into the EU, or serving EU customers, carry obligations too. We also work with NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, SR 11-7 style model risk controls and local regulator guidance.

What happens after the build is done?

Either your team takes over with documentation, training and a few weeks of on-call support from us, or we keep running it under a managed operations retainer with service levels and monthly reporting.
