What it does

The full manifold workflow in one tool.

  • 3D block definition with units and material
  • Cavity, drilling and port placement with offset holes
  • Checks for intersections, depth to tip and net connectivity

Who it's for

Teams replacing spreadsheet and desktop-CAD workflows for manifold design.

  • Hydraulic and fluid-power design teams
  • Manifold manufacturers quoting and producing blocks
  • Engineering firms standardising their manifold process

How it's delivered

Zero-install software with your designs kept safe.

  • Runs in the browser, nothing to deploy
  • Designs saved to your account and per browser
  • Reports and downloads for manufacturing
