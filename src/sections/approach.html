Small scopes, measurable outcomes

Every engagement follows the same four moves. It keeps risk low for you and keeps us accountable for what is working.

  1. 1

    Frame

    Agree the business outcome, the metric and what good enough looks like before any code is written.

  2. 2

    Prototype

    A working slice on your real data in weeks, not quarters, with an evaluation set you can inspect.

  3. 3

    Harden

    Guardrails, monitoring, cost controls, compliance evidence and fallbacks so the system behaves in production.

  4. 4

    Hand over or operate

    Your team owns it with documentation and training, or we keep running it under a managed retainer.
