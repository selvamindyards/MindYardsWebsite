02 · Agentic AI

Agents that do the work, inside processes you can still control

Agentic AI is at the top of every roadmap and execution is the bottleneck. We design agents around a real business process, give them the tools and data they need, put a human at the right checkpoints, and instrument everything so you know what they did and why.

Typical scope
Pilot in 6 to 8 weeks, then scale in phases
Best for
High-volume, rules-heavy work with clear hand-off points
Frameworks
Open frameworks you can own, Azure AI Foundry, Agentforce, or your own platform
Discuss an agent pilot

Multi-agent systems

Agents that plan, call tools, and hand off to each other or to a person, with explicit contracts for what each agent may do.

  • Planner, worker and reviewer patterns
  • Tool and data access scoped per agent
  • Human approval steps where risk demands them

Workflow orchestration

Agents embedded in real business processes such as case handling, approvals, onboarding and order-to-cash, not standalone chat windows.

  • Process mapping and redesign before automation
  • Integration with workflow, ticketing and ERP systems
  • Exception handling and escalation design

AgentOps & monitoring

Observability, guardrails, tracing and cost controls so every agent action is logged, every failure is visible and spend stays predictable.

  • Trace-level logging and replay
  • Policy guardrails on inputs, tools and outputs
  • Cost, latency and quality dashboards

Agent lifecycle management

Versioning, access, ownership and retirement for a growing fleet of agents, so they stay governed rather than sprawling across the business.

  • Agent registry and ownership model
  • Change control, testing and release gates
  • Decommissioning and knowledge retention
