Product · Insurance Advisor CRM

A CRM built around the adviser's day, not the database

Insurance Advisor CRM is a MindYards product for insurance and protection advisers. It manages enquiries to completion with an SLA-driven work queue, document chasing, AI-extracted client data and commission reconciliation, synced with Microsoft 365.

Who it's for
Insurance and protection advisers, broker firms and adviser networks
Format
Live web application with demo data, tenant setup and customisation on request

Microsoft 365 calendar and mail, with provider and payroll feeds on request

What it does

One screen for the work that matters today.

  • Pipeline by stage with follow-up and document SLAs
  • Parties, cases, tasks and appointments in one view
  • Earnings expected versus reconciled, with clawback visibility

AI inside

AI that removes admin, with the adviser approving every result.

  • Extraction of payslips and documents into case fields for review
  • Suggested next actions in the work queue
  • Natural-language search across parties and cases
