Turn sensor data, drawings and quality records into uptime and yield

Plants already generate the data AI needs: machine logs, inspection images, maintenance records, SOPs and drawings. We connect it, build the models and put the results in front of operators and planners inside the tools they already use.

Predictive maintenance

Failure prediction and remaining-useful-life models on sensor and maintenance data, with work orders raised in your CMMS before the breakdown.

  • Works with existing historians
  • Alerts tuned to planner capacity
  • Measured in avoided downtime

Visual quality control

Camera-based defect detection trained on your parts and your defects, deployed at the line with operator feedback loops.

  • Edge or on-premises inference
  • Defect libraries you own
  • Escape-rate reporting

Production planning & scheduling

Optimisation and forecasting that balance orders, capacity, changeovers and material availability, with planners in control of the final schedule.

  • Constraint-based scheduling
  • Demand and material forecasts
  • What-if scenarios for planners

Technician copilots on SOPs & manuals

Assistants that answer from your SOPs, manuals and past work orders, on the shop floor, in the technician's language.

  • Retrieval over manuals and drawings
  • Voice and mobile access
  • Feedback into document updates
