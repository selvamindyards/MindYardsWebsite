Radiology & pathology triage support

Worklist prioritisation and finding detection that puts urgent studies in front of the right reader first, with the radiologist always making the call.

  • Integration with PACS and RIS
  • Audit trail for every flag
  • Measured against reader agreement

Clinical notes & discharge summaries

Ambient and dictation-based drafting that turns consultations into structured notes and discharge summaries for clinician review.

  • Specialty-specific templates
