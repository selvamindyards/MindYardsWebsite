03 · Managed AI operations

We run it after launch, so your team doesn't have to

Models drift, prompts regress, token bills grow and agents need tending. Our managed operations service keeps your AI accurate, affordable and compliant on a monthly retainer, with named SLAs and a report you can take to your leadership.

Format
Monthly retainer, quarterly review, cancel with notice
Best for
Anything in production that has to stay reliable without a full-time internal team
Includes
Monitoring, on-call, improvements, monthly reporting
Ask about a retainer

Model monitoring & retraining

Drift, accuracy and data-quality monitoring against agreed SLAs, with scheduled retraining, validation and sign-off.

  • Performance and drift alerts with thresholds you set
  • Retraining pipelines with champion/challenger tests
  • Monthly model health report

LLMOps / AI FinOps

Token spend, prompt versioning, evaluation regression and model routing managed as one discipline, reported monthly.

  • Prompt and eval regression suites on every change
  • Caching, routing and model-tier optimisation
  • Spend forecasting and chargeback by team

Agents as a service

Done-for-you agents on a monthly retainer. We build, run, tune and improve them. You consume the outcome, measured in tickets resolved, documents processed or calls handled.

  • Outcome-based scope and pricing
  • Continuous tuning from production feedback
  • Human-in-the-loop coverage where needed

AI-first IT managed services

Service desk, document handling and back-office operations run by AI with people in the loop, under a single contract with service levels.

  • L1 support and request handling by agents
  • Document and email operations
  • Escalation to your team or ours
