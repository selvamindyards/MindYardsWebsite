05 · Governance, risk & compliance

Audit-ready AI for regulated businesses

Regulators, auditors and enterprise customers now ask for AI controls by name. We help banks, insurers, healthcare providers and public bodies classify their systems, close the gaps and produce evidence that stands up to scrutiny.

Typical scope
4 to 10 weeks for a gap assessment; recurring for assurance
Best for
Regulated sectors, vendors selling AI into them, and anyone facing the EU AI Act
Frameworks
EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, SR 11-7, RBI and local guidance
Request a gap assessment

EU AI Act readiness

Classification of your AI systems, obligation mapping and the technical documentation, logging and oversight that high-risk use requires.

  • System inventory and risk classification
  • Gap assessment against current obligations
  • Technical files, conformity evidence, transparency notices

NIST AI RMF / ISO 42001

Framework mapping, policy sets and an AI management system built to pass certification audits and customer due diligence.

  • Policy and control library
  • AI management system implementation
  • Internal audit and certification support

AI audit & assurance

Independent review of models and agents: lineage, audit trails, performance evidence and attestations that your auditors and customers will accept.

  • Model and agent audits with written findings
  • Lineage and audit-trail design
  • Third-party attestation support

Red-teaming, bias & safety

Adversarial testing, bias measurement and safety evaluations before launch and on a schedule after, with fixes, not just findings.

  • Prompt injection, jailbreak and data-leak testing
  • Fairness testing across protected groups
  • Safety evals built into your release process

Model risk management

SR 11-7 style model inventories, independent validation and ongoing controls for banks, insurers and lenders, extended to cover LLMs and agents.

  • Model inventory and tiering
  • Validation reports and challenger models
  • Ongoing monitoring and committee reporting
